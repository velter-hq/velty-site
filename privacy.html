Privacy Policy

Updated: 2 November 2026

In short: The Velty mobile app does not send your personal data to our servers. Everything Velty knows stays on your phone (iPhone or Android). There is no account, no advertising and no tracking.

Who we are

Velty is made by VELTER KZ. Contact: hello@velter.co.

What stays on your phone

To work, Velty stores the following on your device only:

  • your settings (pause length, pause mode, schedule, theme);
  • pause statistics: how many pauses you had, how many times you changed your mind or opened an app, and the optional “why” answers;
  • the list of apps you chose. On iPhone, Apple’s Screen Time picker gives Velty only anonymous tokens for these apps — Velty cannot see which apps they are or what you do in them. On Android, Velty keeps the package names of the apps you picked;
  • if you use Velty Tag: the ID of your NFC tag and how long apps stayed locked by it.

This data is not sent to us or to anyone else. You can erase statistics in Velty → Settings → Reset statistics, or remove all data by deleting the app.

Apple features Velty uses

  • Screen Time (Family Controls). Used only on your device to show Apple’s pause screen over the apps you chose and to know when the time you picked is up. Velty does not receive your usage history.
  • Shortcuts. Your automation tells Velty which app you are opening, so it can show the pause. This stays on the device.
  • Live Activities. The breathing ring in the Dynamic Island during a pause.
  • NFC (Velty Tag). Velty reads only your tag’s ID to lock or unlock the apps you chose. Nothing is written to the tag.

Android: what Velty uses

  • Accessibility service. Velty uses the Android accessibility service only to know which app is opened (its package name) and to show the pause before it. Velty does not read screen content, taps or text, and does not keep a history of other apps outside your phone.
  • NFC. Only to read your Velty Tag’s ID.
  • Notifications. Only a silent notification with the minutes left after you open an app — no reminders.
  • No internet permission. The Android app cannot connect to the internet, so nothing can leave your phone. Data is excluded from Android cloud backups and device-to-device transfer.

What we don’t do

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No reminders: Velty does not send push notifications or local reminders (on Android, only the silent countdown described above).
  • No analytics or advertising SDKs, no tracking across apps or websites.
  • We do not sell app data or transfer it from your device.

Links

Velty has a link to the Velter website. If you open it, that website’s own privacy policy applies.

Children

The Velty mobile app does not collect personal data from anyone, including children.

Website waitlist

This section covers the website form, separately from mobile app data. When you join the waitlist, we store your email address, page language, submission source, signup time and consent version. We use this information to send Velty launch and product availability news.

Your address is saved only after you submit the form and agree to receive news. The list is stored on our server outside the public website files. Access to the list and CSV export is password protected. We do not sell the list or publish addresses. The form does not connect advertising or analytics services.

We keep your signup while it is needed for launch news or until you ask us to remove it. To remove your address or withdraw consent, email hello@velter.co from that address. You can also contact us to ask what signup information we hold about you.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date. If Velty ever starts sending any data off the device, we will ask for your consent first.
